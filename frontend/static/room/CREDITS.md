# Showcase room asset credits

All assets are public domain (CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), from
Poly Haven. Attribution is not required; we credit the authors anyway. Models are the 1k glTF
downloads packed to GLB with `@gltf-transform/cli copy` (no Draco/KTX2). Loaded by
`src/lib/room-showcase/scene.ts` for the `/plans/room` page. The sofa reuses
`static/models/sofa.glb` (Poly Haven "Sofa 02", credited in `static/models/CREDITS.md`), re-dyed
cream in a shader.
